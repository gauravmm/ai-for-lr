GRANT-02 §1 — The ceiling includes every mandatory part of a responsive offer. Applicable GST is excluded from the M1 ceiling comparison and must be identified separately in commercial responses.

## GRANT-02 §2 Basis of allocation

GRANT-02 §2.1 — The category amounts provide an internal planning breakdown for the complete equipment acquisition. The total is the controlling ceiling; an individual line is not a separate authorisation to omit a required item. Bidders may distribute their quoted prices differently across hardware and service lines, so evaluation should reconcile the included scope before comparing the total.

GRANT-02 §2.2 — The price basis is Singapore dollars excluding GST. Any applicable tax should be shown separately and retained in the purchase record, but the equipment ceiling and RFQ mandatory price gate use the excluding-GST figure. The reviewer should identify arithmetic differences between the sum of quoted components and the stated total, and seek a process-approved resolution before relying on an inconsistent figure.

## GRANT-02 §3 Cost evidence

GRANT-02 §3.1 — The budget file should be read with the grant agreement and the tender's commercial response form. The purchase case should identify the instrument configuration, mandatory options, installation and Site Acceptance Test, eight-person training, and the warranty, maintenance and response service that spans the project need. A quote for a possible later extension is neither an approved allocation nor part of an evaluated total unless it forms part of the submitted offer.
