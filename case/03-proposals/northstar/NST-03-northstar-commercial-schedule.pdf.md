## NST-03 §3 Quoted-price basis

NST-03 §3.1 — The submitted total of S$348,000 is in Singapore dollars excluding GST and applies to the configuration stated in NST-02. The line items distinguish equipment and controls from installation, acceptance, eight-person training and the signed service obligation. The C6 figure is the total for tender comparison; no allowance should be added or removed without checking the item to which it relates.

NST-03 §3.2 — The C5 line prices the 30-month warranty and service period specified in NST-05. A changed commencement basis or additional period would be a separate commercial change and has no approved tender price in this schedule. The submitted total must be read with that signed term.
