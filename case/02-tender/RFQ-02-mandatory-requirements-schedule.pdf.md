## RFQ-02 §2 Reading the schedule

RFQ-02 §2.1 — The requirement IDs remain stable across the tender, bidder responses and evaluation records. A response should identify a concrete submitted passage for each claimed compliance result. A numerical total alone cannot establish the included scope, and an implementation date alone cannot establish the duration of warranty or technical response. Read the main response with the relevant signed appendix.

RFQ-02 §2.2 — The gate is conjunctive: every mandatory item must be satisfied before a bid receives a weighted rank. The evaluator should record the observed value, evidence status and any calculation used. Where a capability, price or service condition depends on a later option, the submitted base offer must be assessed as written. Distinguish an explanation of existing wording from an amendment that adds an obligation.

## RFQ-02 §3 Evidence conventions

RFQ-02 §3.1 — For M1, use the complete evaluated price excluding GST and reconcile it with required inclusions. For M2, use the committed Site Acceptance Test date rather than shipment or installation. For M3 and M4, use the delivered technical configuration and export capability. M5 and M6 require a read across commercial, implementation and service records. M7 asks whether a claimed mandatory obligation actually entered the submitted tender record by close.

RFQ-02 §3.2 — Record a failed or unresolved gate with the exact source passage, not a general impression of the supplier. A clarification may be relevant to interpretation, but its status must be retained in the evidence chain. The gate result should be reproducible by another reviewer using the same documents and dates.
